# Three-minute demo video: script and storyboard

Use this as the script for your required demo video. It maps directly to the judging rubric, so following it earns points. Keep to three minutes; going over reads as unprepared.

Replace the bracketed parts with your content.

## Timing and shot list

| Time | Section | What is on screen | What you say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:20 | Hook and beneficiary | Title slide | "Meet [named person]. Today, [their problem]." |
| 0:20-0:35 | The claim | Problem slide | "Our agent [measurable claim, with a number]." |
| 0:35-2:15 | Live demo (the aha) | Live agent, then the trace | Do the real thing: [the one aha moment]. Narrate briefly while the agent works. Then show the observability trace and say "notice it made these calls on its own." |
| 2:15-2:40 | Impact and honesty | Impact slide | "This helps [beneficiary] by [claim]. What is mocked: [X]. What we would harden next: [Y]." |
| 2:40-3:00 | Close | Closing slide | "Team [name]. Thank you." |

## Recording tips

- Record the live agent, not slides reading. The aha moment is the agent doing the work.
- For the guardrailed track, show the refusal on camera. A clean decline is memorable.
- Reply in the user's language where relevant, and show the right-to-left interface, to collect regional-relevance points.
- Rehearse once and time it. The first take always runs long.

## Format

- Length: 3 minutes maximum.
- File: MP4 (H.264), 1080p, under the size limit the submission system states.
- Audio: clear narration; avoid background noise.
- Captions help judges and support accessibility.

## Where to submit

Upload the video with your other deliverables in the submission system (see the Submit and Present page). Name it `team-<yourteam>-demo.mp4`.
